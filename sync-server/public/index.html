💡 How to choose folder/path on phone? ▼
🤖 Android (Chrome) 1. Tap the 3-dots menu (top right) in Chrome.
2. Go to Settings → Downloads.
3. Toggle on "Ask where to save files".
Now Chrome will ask you to choose a folder on your phone before every download.
🍎 iOS iPhone (Safari) 1. Open the phone's Settings app.
2. Tap Safari → Downloads.
3. Select "Other..." to pick any custom folder in your Files app (or select "Ask each time").
